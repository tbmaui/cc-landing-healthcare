The Problem

Patient care comes first. The books still have to get done.

Running a medical practice, med spa, dental office, dermatology clinic or chiropractic practice means juggling patient care, insurance reimbursements, compliance requirements and overhead, all while keeping your finances in order.

Insurance companies pay slowly. Claims get denied and need follow-up. Equipment, supplies and repairs add up. When the books fall behind, it gets hard to see what's coming in, what's still owed and whether you can cover next month.

Complete Controller handles the bookkeeping so you can stay with your patients.

Your team posts and reconciles information from your medical billing provider, keeps receivables represented accurately on the balance sheet and monitors practice expenses.

Questions

What practice owners ask us.

How much does bookkeeping for a medical practice cost?

Most single-location practices fit Basic at $405 a month. Practices with more than one location, or with investors or lenders, usually fit Complete at $970 a month. Each plan has a one-time setup fee, and you'll know the price before you commit.

Can you help track reimbursement delays from insurance companies?

We post and reconcile your medical billing company reports so reimbursements and outstanding receivables are accurately reflected in your books. Your billing provider manages claim follow-up and collections.

Can you help with patient billing and revenue cycle management?

We do not provide medical billing, submit claims or manage collections. We post and reconcile information from your medical billing provider so those activities are represented accurately in your books and on the balance sheet.

Can you report on earnings and costs for multiple partners?

Yes. Our Premier and Deluxe packages support complex practices that need to track earnings and costs for multiple partners. We discuss your ownership structure and reporting needs during the consultation.

Do you work with our practice management and billing software?

Yes. We work with your existing tools, whether that's QuickBooks, practice management or claim-handling systems, to keep disruption to a minimum.

Is our patient and financial data safe?

Yes. Our systems are cloud-based and encrypted, with strict access controls, and our processes are designed to meet audit-ready standards for healthcare practices.

We already have internal accounting staff. Can you support or review their work?

Yes. Many healthcare clients use our oversight services to catch errors, tighten financial controls and check billing accuracy.

How quickly can we get set up?

Most practices are onboarded and running smoothly within 1 to 2 weeks, depending on size and complexity.
